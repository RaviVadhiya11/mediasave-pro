Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. What MediaSave Pro does

MediaSave Pro is a static website that helps a visitor download a direct media-file URL when the remote host permits browser access. It does not provide a server-side downloader or fetch social-media post pages.

2. Information and download history

The website does not require an account. If you use the download history feature, the website stores a small list of file names and timestamps in local storage in your own browser. This information is not sent to a MediaSave Pro server. You can clear it using the Clear history button or your browser's site-data settings.

3. Third-party websites

When you download a file, your browser connects directly to the URL you entered. The destination website may receive your IP address and other information according to its own privacy policy. MediaSave Pro does not control third-party sites.

4. Cookies and analytics

This starter project does not include advertising cookies or analytics trackers. If you add third-party analytics or advertising later, update this policy and obtain any consent required by applicable law.

5. Children's privacy

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit personal or sensitive information through third-party links.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when website features change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page. Replace the sample contact address there with an email address you control before publishing.
